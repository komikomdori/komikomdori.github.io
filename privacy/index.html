Privacy and Google OAuth

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 16 November 2026 · Operator: KOMDORI Report Drive

1. Purpose

KOMDORI Report Drive is a privately operated integration for storing and verifying personal reporting files using an authorized Google Drive account.

2. Google user data accessed

After Google OAuth consent, the integration may process Google Drive file identifiers, folder metadata, sharing/permission metadata, and file contents needed for storage and verification of report images and application-managed backup files. The exact access available is limited by the scopes granted during Google authorization.

3. Other information processed

Reports may contain exercise, nutrition, sleep, and recovery information obtained from separate user-authorized sources. This information is used to prepare the personal reports and is not collected from visitors to this informational website.

4. How the data is used

  • Generate and store personal reports and application-managed backup files.
  • Locate and verify files, including checking successful uploads.
  • Operate the user's scheduled personal reporting workflow.

Google user data is not sold and is not used for targeted advertising. This website does not intentionally use analytics or tracking cookies.

5. Storage, transfers and sharing

Personal report files are intended to stay in access-restricted Google Drive storage. GitHub Actions is used to run the private automation workflow, and separately authorized KakaoTalk messaging may be used to deliver report references to the operator's configured account. The public informational website does not host or display private health-report files.

The integration does not intentionally make Google Drive reports publicly shared. Access also depends on the permissions configured in the Google account and applicable service settings.

6. Security

Authentication tokens and private automation configuration are stored separately from this public informational website. Authenticated API access and post-upload verification are used where configured. No online system can guarantee absolute security.

7. Retention and deletion

Files remain in the configured storage accounts until deleted by the operator according to their account settings and operational needs. Temporary automation artifacts may also be retained for limited periods. Revoking Google authorization prevents future authorized access but does not automatically remove already stored files.

The operator can manage or delete report files in their Google Drive account and can revoke OAuth authorization in Google Account security settings.

8. Changes

This policy may change if data access, reporting functionality, or processing practices change. Updated text and its effective date will be published here.

9. Contact

For privacy inquiries or data removal requests, contact: komi.komdori@gmail.com.
